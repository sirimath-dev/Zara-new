Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions.

How do I connect my WhatsApp?

Generate a pairing code, open WhatsApp → Linked Devices → Link with phone number and enter your code.

How do I open the dashboard?

Use the Dashboard button and log in using the credentials provided by your bot backend.

What is Anti Delete?

It is a feature that can notify you about deleted messages where supported by your bot configuration. Respect other people's privacy.

Can I save WhatsApp statuses?

Available status tools depend on your bot configuration and the permissions of your WhatsApp account.

Does Queen Zara support multiple sessions?

The website supports a multi-session interface. Actual multi-session support must be implemented in the backend.

Is Queen Zara free?

The website is free to use. Hosting and backend services may have separate costs.

How can I disconnect my bot?

Use the disconnect option in your dashboard or remove the linked device directly from WhatsApp.
